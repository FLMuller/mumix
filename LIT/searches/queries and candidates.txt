   diameter about 10 eta (near wall) and 8.5 eta (log region), maximum azimuthal velocity 2.0 and 1.7 u_K, independent of Re_tau.
Wang, Tanahashi and Miyauchi (2007) Int. J. Heat Fluid Flow 28, 1280. DOI 10.1016/j.ijheatfluidflow.2007.06.009. Mixing layer
   DNS (Re_omega0 = 700): diameter about 8 eta, maximum azimuthal velocity 2.0 u_K falling to 1.2 u_K.

Ishihara, Kaneda and Hunt (2013) Flow Turb. Combust. 91, 895. DOI 10.1007/s10494-013-9499-z. Re_lambda up to 1131: vortical
