Table 1 of the paper (a compilation of IVS data from the literature) is an image and was not read, and the circulation Reynolds number quoted there uses a different definition (\(Re_\Gamma Re_\lambda^{-1/2}\approx28\)), so it is not used. Their \(u_0/u'\) (0.7–0.8) is lower than that of Ghira et al. (1.0–1.25).
