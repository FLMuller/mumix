Remarks in the page: The authors say \(R_0\) is larger and \(V_0\) smaller than the true means, because off-centre and inclined tubes are included. The radius and the swirl are corrected (A0c: radius divided by 1.75, swirl multiplied by 3) and both are fitted; the listed \(Re_0\) is not fitted, the tube Reynolds number follows from the corrected values.

3. The conversions
11
$$r_{\max}=R_0,\qquad \dfrac{U_{\max}}{U_\eta}=\dfrac{V_0}{u_K},\qquad Re_c=Re_0=\dfrac{R_0V_0}{\nu}$$
12
The Burgers vortex: \(x=1.2564312\) is the root of \(e^{x}-2x-1=0\), \(K_B=1-e^{-x}=0.71531\), \(r_{\max}=\sqrt{x}\,R_B=1.1209\,R_B\), \(U_{\max}=K_B\,\Gamma/(2\pi r_{\max})\), so \(\Gamma/\nu=(2\pi/K_B)\,Re_c=8.784\,Re_c\) with \(Re_c=r_{\max}U_{\max}/\nu\).

Worked example, the first row (\(Re_\lambda\) = 332). The model side needs lengths in metres, so \(\eta\) is made from a \(\nu\) and an \(\varepsilon\) (measured in the paper):
16
$$\eta=\left(\frac{\nu^3}{\varepsilon}\right)^{1/4}=\left(\frac{(1.41e-05)^3}{0.0332}\right)^{1/4}=0.0005391\ \mathrm{m}=539.1\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
17
$$U_\eta=(\nu\varepsilon)^{1/4}=0.02616\ \mathrm{m/s}=26.16\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
18
$$r_{\max}=\beta\,\eta=4.829\times539.1\ \mu\mathrm{m}=2603\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
19
$$U_{\max}=U\,U_\eta=7.290\times26.16\ \mathrm{mm/s}=190.7\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
20
$$\gamma=\frac{4x}{\beta^2}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{\nu}\right)^{1/2}=\frac{4\times1.2564}{4.829^2}\left(\frac{0.0332}{1.41e-05}\right)^{1/2}=10.46\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$$
