Open markers are found as holes: the image is binarised, the holes of the closed marker outlines are filled, the dark pixels (the black lines and filled markers) are subtracted, and what remains is a ring. A ring with too much dark inside it (a hole between two black markers, not a marker) is rejected (annulus darkness above 0.60). The marker positions then lie on a grid of the x positions of the series: where a marker is hidden behind a black one, the expected x position is taken from the two series' spacing and the pixels there are averaged in y.
