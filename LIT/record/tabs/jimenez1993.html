The tracking algorithm (page 79 of the journal). (Jimenez (1993), page 15 of the pdf)
10
Read from the page: 'the vorticity in the normal plane is averaged azimuthally, and the resulting radial distribution is fitted to a Gaussian. The local radius of the worm is defined as the 1/e radius of the Gaussian, and the circulation as that of the fitted distribution.' A worm is followed until its peak vorticity falls below \(\omega'\). So \(R\) is the Burgers radius and \(Re_\gamma\) the total circulation. [checked on the page image]
