3. How the eddies are found
9
The identification of the paper: the local flow pattern, no threshold.
10
The scheme (from the text): Q of the velocity gradient tensor at each collocation point; the probability that a positive maximum of Q lies near each point; the points with high probability are searched with a 3-D fourth-order Lagrange interpolation for the true maximum; a plane normal to the vorticity vector through the maximum gives the mean azimuthal velocity, and the point of minimum variance of the azimuthal velocity is searched around it. The radius is where the mean azimuthal velocity is maximal; \(\eta\) and \(u_K\) are those 'calculated from mean energy dissipation rate at \(y^+\), where the eddy exists'.

5. Digitising
16
The pdf is drawn with a logarithmic vertical axis, and the curves of the figure are a band (several curves). The digitiser finds, at each horizontal position, the upper bound and the lower bound of the plotted curves, and takes \(P=(P_{top}+P_{bottom})/2\). The pixel rows are turned into values with the tick marks of the axis: \(\log_{10}P = \text{top exponent} - \dfrac{y-y_0}{y_1-y_0}\times\text{decades}\) (a straight line on the logarithmic axis, fixed by two labelled ticks). Here the band is the nine curves of the nine wall bands: the result is the pdf of the tubes over all bands, with the bands' spread as the width of the band.
