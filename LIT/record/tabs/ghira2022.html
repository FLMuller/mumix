Remarks in the page: Their \(\Gamma_{ivs}=2\pi\langle U_{ivs}\rangle R_{ivs}\) (Eq. 9) is the circulation inside \(R_{ivs}\), about 61% of the total of a Burgers vortex, and is about 0.83 of \(2\pi U_{ivs}R_{ivs}\) from the same table (162 against 136 at \(Re_\lambda=90\)), probably because the paper averages the product per structure. The total circulation is therefore taken from the converted radius and swirl, \(\Gamma/\nu=8.78\,Re_c\) with \(Re_c=(r_{\max}/\eta)(U_{\max}/U_\eta)\); the listed \(Re_\Gamma Re_\lambda^{-1/2}\) is kept in the table as the source value. The swirl row converts the swirl listed at \(R_{ivs}\) to the Burgers peak; the tubes are intense, so \(Re_c\) is about twice that of the whole tubes.
