Remarks in the page: These are the intense tubes of the same experiments as Mouri and Hori (2009): at the 0.1% threshold \(R_0=5.2\)–6.2 \(\eta\), \(V_0=7.6\)–14 \(u_K\) (0.6–0.8 of the r.m.s. velocity) and \(Re_0=39\)–87; at 1%, \(R_0=6.4\)–7.4, \(V_0=5.6\)–9.2 and \(Re_0=37\)–65. The swirl scales with the r.m.s. velocity and \(Re_0\propto Re_\lambda^{1/2}\) (their Fig. 7).

3. The conversions
11
$$r_{\max}=R_0,\qquad \dfrac{U_{\max}}{U_\eta}=\dfrac{V_0}{u_K},\qquad Re_c=Re_0=\dfrac{R_0V_0}{\nu}$$
12
The Burgers vortex: \(x=1.2564312\) is the root of \(e^{x}-2x-1=0\), \(K_B=1-e^{-x}=0.71531\), \(r_{\max}=\sqrt{x}\,R_B=1.1209\,R_B\), \(U_{\max}=K_B\,\Gamma/(2\pi r_{\max})\), so \(\Gamma/\nu=(2\pi/K_B)\,Re_c=8.784\,Re_c\) with \(Re_c=r_{\max}U_{\max}/\nu\).

Worked example, the first row (\(Re_\lambda\) = 332). The model side needs lengths in metres, so \(\eta\) is made from a \(\nu\) and an \(\varepsilon\) (measured in the paper):
16
$$\eta=\left(\frac{\nu^3}{\varepsilon}\right)^{1/4}=\left(\frac{(1.41e-05)^3}{0.405}\right)^{1/4}=0.0002884\ \mathrm{m}=288.4\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
17
$$U_\eta=(\nu\varepsilon)^{1/4}=0.04888\ \mathrm{m/s}=48.88\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
18
$$r_{\max}=\beta\,\eta=5.180\times288.4\ \mu\mathrm{m}=1494\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
19
$$U_{\max}=U\,U_\eta=7.600\times48.88\ \mathrm{mm/s}=371.5\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
20
$$\gamma=\frac{4x}{\beta^2}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{\nu}\right)^{1/2}=\frac{4\times1.2564}{5.180^2}\left(\frac{0.405}{1.41e-05}\right)^{1/2}=9.089\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$$
