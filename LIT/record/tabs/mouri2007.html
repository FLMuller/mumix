Agreed use: not used in the model. The 0.1% and 1% averages are two tails of the chord-length distribution of the same experiments as Mouri and Hori (2009): a high threshold keeps the near-central, near-normal crossings, so the radius is smaller and the swirl larger than the all-event average, by an amount that depends on the threshold. They cannot be corrected row by row, because each holds only part of the distribution; the 2009 paper, which averages the whole distribution, is used instead (corrected). The paper itself makes no chord correction: that work is ours (tab Corrections). The Burgers vortex: \(x=1.2564312\) is the root of \(e^{x}-2x-1=0\), \(K_B=1-e^{-x}=0.71531\), \(r_{\max}=\sqrt{x}\,R_B=1.1209\,R_B\), \(U_{\max}=K_B\,\Gamma/(2\pi r_{\max})\), so \(\Gamma/\nu=(2\pi/K_B)\,Re_c=8.784\,Re_c\) with \(Re_c=r_{\max}U_{\max}/\nu\).

24
$$\eta=\left(\frac{\nu^3}{\varepsilon}\right)^{1/4}=\left(\frac{(1.41e-05)^3}{0.405}\right)^{1/4}=0.0002884\ \mathrm{m}=288.4\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
25
$$U_\eta=(\nu\varepsilon)^{1/4}=0.04888\ \mathrm{m/s}=48.88\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
26
$$r_{\max}=\beta\,\eta=5.180\times288.4\ \mu\mathrm{m}=1494\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
27
$$U_{\max}=U\,U_\eta=7.600\times48.88\ \mathrm{mm/s}=371.5\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
28
$$\gamma=\frac{4x}{\beta^2}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{\nu}\right)^{1/2}=\frac{4\times1.2564}{5.180^2}\left(\frac{0.405}{1.41e-05}\right)^{1/2}=9.089\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$$
