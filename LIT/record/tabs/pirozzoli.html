7. Digitising
29
The pdf is drawn with a logarithmic vertical axis, and the curves of the figure are a band (several curves). The digitiser finds, at each horizontal position, the upper bound and the lower bound of the plotted curves, and takes \(P=(P_{top}+P_{bottom})/2\). The pixel rows are turned into values with the tick marks of the axis: \(\log_{10}P = \text{top exponent} - \dfrac{y-y_0}{y_1-y_0}\times\text{decades}\) (a straight line on the logarithmic axis, fixed by two labelled ticks). Here the plotted DNS circles and the Tanahashi line are two series on one figure; our curve follows the markers (open circles) and the line where the circles are absent (the tail beyond r0/η ≈ 35).
