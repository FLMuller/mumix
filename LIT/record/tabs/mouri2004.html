3. The conversions

$$r_{\max}=R_0,\qquad \dfrac{U_{\max}}{U_\eta}=\dfrac{V_0}{u_K},\qquad Re_c=Re_0=\dfrac{R_0V_0}{\nu}$$
11
The Burgers vortex: \(x=1.2564312\) is the root of \(e^{x}-2x-1=0\), \(K_B=1-e^{-x}=0.71531\), \(r_{\max}=\sqrt{x}\,R_B=1.1209\,R_B\), \(U_{\max}=K_B\,\Gamma/(2\pi r_{\max})\), so \(\Gamma/\nu=(2\pi/K_B)\,Re_c=8.784\,Re_c\) with \(Re_c=r_{\max}U_{\max}/\nu\).

Worked example, the first row (\(Re_\lambda\) = 295). The model side needs lengths in metres, so \(\eta\) is made from a \(\nu\) and an \(\varepsilon\) (measured in the paper):
15
$$\eta=\left(\frac{\nu^3}{\varepsilon}\right)^{1/4}=\left(\frac{(1.44e-05)^3}{0.0316}\right)^{1/4}=0.0005544\ \mathrm{m}=554.4\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
16
$$U_\eta=(\nu\varepsilon)^{1/4}=0.02597\ \mathrm{m/s}=25.97\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
17
$$r_{\max}=\beta\,\eta=6.080\times554.4\ \mu\mathrm{m}=3371\ \mu\mathrm{m}$$
18
$$U_{\max}=U\,U_\eta=5.230\times25.97\ \mathrm{mm/s}=135.8\ \mathrm{mm/s}$$
19
$$\gamma=\frac{4x}{\beta^2}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{\nu}\right)^{1/2}=\frac{4\times1.2564}{6.080^2}\left(\frac{0.0316}{1.44e-05}\right)^{1/2}=6.369\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$$
