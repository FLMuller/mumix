7. The method applied to the model tubes
28
The model of the tubes is the wire-and-tube model of the Belin tab, with the sensed quantity changed: a crossed wire gives the transverse velocity (the spanwise \(v\) of Mouri, the vertical \(w\) of Sassa), which is linear in the swirl and needs no incidence angle. Straight Burgers tubes with isotropic axes and uniform offsets are crossed along the path of the mean flow, the signal is smoothed by the probe (a boxcar of 1 mm for Mouri, about \(\eta\) for Sassa), the thresholds are in units of the rms increment \(\sqrt{2/15}\,(U_\eta/\eta)\,\delta x\) of the dissipative range, and a tube is weighted by the distance within which it can be detected. There is no background turbulence in the model (script chord_mouri.py).
29
Method of the paper: the increment \(v(x+\delta x_s/2)-v(x-\delta x_s/2)\) over the sampling interval \(\delta x_s\); the events above the threshold that holds 0.1% or 1% of all increments (the threshold levels of Table II of Mouri et al. 2007: 6.0–6.7 and 3.5–3.7 standard deviations); the signals are aligned on the increment, given the same sign, averaged, and the mean profile is fitted around its peaks by the Burgers vortex for \(x_0=y_0=\theta_0=0\) to give \(R_0\) and \(V_0\).
